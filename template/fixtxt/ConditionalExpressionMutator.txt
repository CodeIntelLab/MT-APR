FP6. Mutate Conditional Expression. Mutating a conditional expression that returns a boolean value (i.e., true or false) by either
updating it, or removing a sub conditional expression, or inserting
a new conditional expression into it.
FP6.1: - ...condExp1...
+ ...condExp2...
FP6.2: - ...condExp1 Op condExp2...
+ ...condExp1...
FP6.3: - ...condExp1...
+ ...condExp1 Op condExp2...
